# LiquidityPool.app — SEO Launch Plan

## Editorial Positioning

LiquidityPool.app is positioned as an **independent, English-language DeFi research publication** focused on the mechanisms, risks, and research methods behind decentralized liquidity. The site avoids price calls and product endorsements. Its advantage is a calm, mechanism-first editorial stance that makes complex concepts legible for serious learners, builders, and liquidity providers.

The initial site architecture intentionally favors a **hub-and-spoke SEO model**. The home page establishes topical authority; the Guides hub distributes link equity to each article; four research tracks clarify information hierarchy; and every article links to same-track reading. This structure supports both human navigation and topical clustering for search engines.

## Internal Linking Rules

Every article should link to at least two closely related same-track guides and one foundational guide when the topic is advanced. The site’s most important pillar URLs are **What Is a Liquidity Pool?**, **Automated Market Makers Explained**, **Impermanent Loss Explained**, and **How to Evaluate a Liquidity Pool**. These pillars receive prominent home-page placement and should remain stable as the library expands.

Future content should follow the existing four-track structure unless a fifth distinct topic cluster reaches at least five related guides. Anchor text should describe the destination topic clearly rather than use generic phrases such as “click here.”

## Technical SEO Included in the Website

The Astro implementation statically generates all guide URLs and includes canonical URLs, semantic article markup, Open Graph and Twitter metadata, organization and article JSON-LD, a generated XML sitemap, a crawlable robots.txt, and an RSS feed. Each article has a unique title, meta description, publication date, keyword theme, and related-reading module. The site is fully static, fast by default, and has no dependency on client-side rendering for its core content.

## Editorial Standards

All content should distinguish explanations of mechanism from financial recommendations. When introducing a new protocol-specific page, cite primary documentation, disclose the assessment date, and include the relevant risks, upgrade controls, dependencies, and incentive assumptions. Claims with time sensitivity should be reviewed every 90 days; evergreen foundations should be refreshed at least annually.

## Next 90-Day Expansion Sequence

After the launch library is indexed, expand one cluster at a time: first protocol-specific explainers for major AMM designs; then comparison guides around AMM curves, LP fee tiers, and stablecoin systems; then calculator-adjacent educational pages that explain inputs and assumptions. Prioritize pages with a clear question-based query pattern and a natural link back to the four launch pillars.
